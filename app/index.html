    About this guide

    Leonida Platinum is a free, open-source, offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no ads. Your checklist progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage); export it to a file to move it to another browser or keep a backup.

    Where the facts come from

    Every trophy's name, description and grade are the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it on 2026-11-18, the two agreeing on all 55. The guides are written for this entry, in our own words, from the official descriptions and the cited sources — chiefly PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, which played the game at launch, and Push Square's guide. Each trophy's guide names its sources beside it; a link opens only when you follow it.

    What is not yet confirmed

    The game released on 2026-11-19, so a few trophies have no confirmed method yet (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their guides say so plainly instead of guessing at a location or a step. Where a method is believed but unconfirmed, the guide says who believes it.
